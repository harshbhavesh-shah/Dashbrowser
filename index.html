Dash

Browser

Allergic to ads. Addicted to speed.

A from-scratch macOS browser built with SwiftUI and WebKit — not a Chromium wrapper. Dash renders every tab through Apple's Liquid Glass and feels like it was actually built for your Mac.

Built on Apple's own frameworks

🗂️

Tabs, groups & pins

Custom-named, colored tab groups, pinned tabs, and a Quick Switcher (⌘K) that searches open tabs and history together.

🛡️

Built-in ad blocking

Native content-rule-list blocking for ads and trackers, plus a dedicated YouTube ad-skip script — toggle it anytime in Settings.

🖼️

A new tab that's yours

Custom background images, editable favorites, a time-aware greeting, and 30 built-in color themes plus a custom accent picker.

✨

Liquid Glass design

Real vibrancy, not a flat tint — the interface adapts to whatever's behind it, the same way Safari's own chrome does.

🔒

Privacy controls

Independent toggles for tracker blocking, history, and search suggestions, private windows locked behind Touch ID, and a choice of search engine.

⚡️

Fast tab switching

A pooled WKWebView architecture keeps tabs warm, so switching between them feels instant even with dozens open.
